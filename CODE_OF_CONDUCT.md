# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Relay a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Do not harass, threaten, demean, publish private information, or use sexualized/discriminatory language. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

This concise policy is based on the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles.
